## Why the split

Everything under [`proofs/`](proofs/) is source code and carries the MIT license in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE), so the tests and models can be reused freely, including in
your own projects.

The reports are a written work rather than software. CC BY-ND keeps them citable
and shareable while preventing edited copies from circulating under the same name,
which matters for a document that makes security claims about a specific commit.
